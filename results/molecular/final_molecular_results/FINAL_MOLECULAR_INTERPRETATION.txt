
PRINOVA — FINAL MOLECULAR INTERPRETATION
=========================================

DATA
----
The molecular experiment used 1,024-dimensional T5 protein embeddings.

REPRESENTATION SELECTION
------------------------
PRINOVA selected PCA with a 256-dimensional latent representation.

Main results:
- PRINOVA score: 0.925
- Reconstruction R2: 0.8914
- Variance explained: 90.96%
- Compression: 1024 -> 256 dimensions

ROBUSTNESS
----------
The 256-dimensional recommendation was reproduced in all three
independent resampling experiments:

- Seed 42: 256 dimensions
- Seed 123: 256 dimensions
- Seed 456: 256 dimensions

Recommendation consistency = 3/3 = 100%.

DOWNSTREAM CLUSTERING
---------------------
The original 256-dimensional PCA + K-means clustering produced a
silhouette score of approximately 0.0759.

A systematic comparison of dimensionality and clustering algorithms
identified 64-dimensional PCA + a two-component Gaussian Mixture Model
as the strongest tested clustering configuration:

- Dimension: 64
- Clusters: 2
- Method: Gaussian Mixture
- Silhouette: 0.1465

This is an improvement in geometric cluster separation, but the
silhouette remains relatively low. Therefore, the clusters should not
be described as strongly separated populations.

BIOLOGICAL VALIDATION
---------------------
A sampled UniProt validation set was used to examine whether the
64-dimensional GMM clusters corresponded to biological annotations.

Two annotations were significant after Benjamini-Hochberg FDR correction:

- ATP binding: FDR q = 0.0486, enriched in Cluster 1
- Toxin activity: FDR q = 0.0486, enriched in Cluster 0

These findings provide evidence of biological differentiation in the
sampled validation set. They should not be generalized to the entire
protein database without a larger independent validation set.

OVERALL CONCLUSION
------------------
The molecular experiment supports PCA-256 as a stable, information-
preserving PRINOVA representation for the 1,024-dimensional protein
embeddings. The downstream analysis additionally suggests that a more
aggressively compressed 64-dimensional representation can expose
stronger geometric cluster structure under a probabilistic clustering
model. Biological annotation analysis provides preliminary evidence
that these clusters are not purely geometric, with two GO annotations
remaining significant after FDR correction.

The representation-selection result and clustering result answer
different questions and should therefore be reported separately rather
than treating 64 dimensions as a replacement for the 256-dimensional
PRINOVA recommendation.
